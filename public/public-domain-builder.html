📚 DNS Configuration Guide

For Replit Deployment:

  1. Go to Deployments tab in Replit
  2. Click "Link a domain" under Settings → Domains
  3. Enter your domain name
  4. Copy the provided A and TXT records
  5. Add records to your domain registrar
  6. Wait for DNS propagation (2-48 hours)
