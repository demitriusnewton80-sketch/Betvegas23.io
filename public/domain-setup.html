📝 Replit Deployment Setup

To connect a custom domain to your Replit deployment:

  1. Go to the Deployments tab in Replit
  2. Under Settings → Domains, click "Link a domain"
  3. Enter your domain name
  4. Add the provided DNS records to your domain registrar
  5. Wait for DNS propagation (usually a few minutes)
  6. Come back here and add your domain using the form above
